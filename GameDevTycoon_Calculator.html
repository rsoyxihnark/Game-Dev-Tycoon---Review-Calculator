Platforms

What does "0.7^n weighting fall-off" mean? (click)
When you ship on multiple platforms, the contribution of each is blended by d *= 0.7 per step (per Platforms.getGenreWeighting):
• 1 platform: contributes 100% of its weight.
• 2 platforms: 1st contributes 70%, 2nd contributes 30%.
• 3 platforms: 1st 70%, 2nd 49%, 3rd −19% (yes, negative: even a perfect-fit 3rd platform hurts the rating).
Rule of thumb: 1 platform is safest. 2 is fine when the 2nd platform's weight is comparable to the 1st (the 2nd still counts for 30%). 3+ almost always lowers the score.

    Where does the score come from?

    1. Topic sets p (topic↔genre raw 0.6..1.0) AND v (topic↔audience raw 0.6..1.0). If p ≤ 0.7 the score above 0.6 is halved → huge effective penalty. 9 topics also override individual mission weights via missionOverrides (Sports, Racing, Mystery, Horror, Detective, Cyberpunk, Law, Music, Rythm).
    2. Genre / Second genre set the golden ratio (Action 1.8 / Sim 1.6 / Strat 1.4 / RPG 0.6 / Casual 0.5 / Adv 0.4; combo = (2·main+second)/3). Tech ≈ Design · ratio. |T − D·ratio| / max(D,T) ≤ 25 % → +0.1 bonus, > 50 % → −0.1.
    3. Platforms give q (normalized weights 0.9..1.1, multi-platform via 0.7^n falloff). 3rd platform contributes negative weight. Wrong audience for a platform applies an additional raw-weight multiplier (when z > 0.7).
    4. Sliders = the 9 dev missions, each with fixed Tech/Design factors and per-genre weights. Across all 9 (not per stage): ≥2 weight≥0.9 missions at ≥40 % → +0.2; weight<0.8 mission at ≥40 % → −0.1 for exactly one, −0.2 for exactly two, nothing for three or more, doubled on an MMO; weight≥0.9 mission at ≤20 % → −0.15 each.
    5. Fatigue = ONLY the immediately previous game with identical topic + genre + secondGenre triggers −0.4, and an expansion pack never takes it.
    6. Engine techLevel matters for Large (need ≥3) and AAA (need ≥5 and 3 experts; 2 experts is psEnabled, which is a different thing). Company office level matters for Small: office level ≥2 (small office / big office / corporate) → 0.9× hidden penalty. Medium with ≤100k fans → 0.9× hidden penalty. (Source: Reviews.f(d,g) uses company.currentLevel for the small branch, NOT flags.techLevel: these are separate fields in codeNw.js.)
    7. Title / Company name change only review flavor text, NEVER the number.
    8. 9→10 ceiling: getting z≥9 requires s ≥ 0.1 AND no fresh hires, plus an internal RNG. Early-game cap drags any 8.5+ down by 0.4..1.5 until you've achieved a top score twice or year ≥ 4.
    9. Reviewer noise: 4 reviewers each pick floor(z) ± {0,1} (occasionally ±2 for 5/6). When the first 3 are all 10 and avg=10, the 4th is biased down by default; with psEnabled (≥2 expert staff matched to weight≥0.9 missions, ANY game size) AND floor(z)=10 the 4th reviewer has a 40% chance to be promoted to 11 instead of demoted.

    See reference/codeNw-review-algorithm.md for the full source dive. All formulas verified against codeNw.js.
